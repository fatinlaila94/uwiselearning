Lesson 1: Is, Am & Are

We use is, am, and are to describe people, things and situations.

📌 Is

Use is with:

He is a student.
She is happy.
It is a book.

📌 Am

Use am with I.

I am a pharmacist.
I am happy.
I am ready.

📌 Are

Use are with:

You are smart.
We are students.
They are happy.

🎯 Remember

I → am
He / She / It → is
You / We / They → are
