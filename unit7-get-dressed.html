02 · Language Focus

What are you wearing?

Use this simple structure to describe the clothes you have on.

Sentence Pattern
I'm wearing + colour + clothes
I'm wearing a blue T-shirt.

Saya memakai baju-T biru.

I'm wearing black trousers.

Saya memakai seluar panjang hitam.

I'm wearing white shoes.

Saya memakai kasut putih.

03 · Everyday Actions

Put it on. Take it off.

Two useful expressions for everyday conversations about clothes.

Action 01

Put on

To start wearing a piece of clothing.

👟 Put on your shoes.
Action 02

Take off

To remove a piece of clothing.

🧥 Take off your jacket.

05 · Watch Out

Small mistakes matter.

Remember to use the correct form when talking about what you are wearing.

✕ Incorrect I wearing a T-shirt.
✓ Correct I'm wearing a T-shirt.
✕ Incorrect I'm wear shoes.
✓ Correct I'm wearing shoes.
