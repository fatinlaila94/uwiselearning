03 · Language Focus

What does it have?

Use has and have to talk about parts and things someone or something owns.

Sentence Pattern It has + noun.
It has two eyes.

Ia mempunyai dua mata.

It has two arms.

Ia mempunyai dua lengan.

It has four wheels.

Ia mempunyai empat roda.

04 · Abilities

What can a robot do?

Use can to talk about things a robot or a person is able to do.

Ability

CAN

Use can before a verb when something is able to do an action.

🤖 The robot can walk.
Negative

CAN'T

Use can't when something is not able to do an action.

🤖 The robot can't fly.
05 · Useful English

Talk about the robot.

Simple questions and answers help you describe a robot naturally.

👩🏻
What is it?
It's a robot.
👦🏻
👩🏻
What does it have?
It has two arms and two legs.
👦🏻
👩🏻
What can it do?
It can walk and move its arms.
👦🏻
06 · Watch Out

Small grammar details.

Watch the verb that comes after can.

✕ Incorrect The robot can walks.
✓ Correct The robot can walk.
✕ Incorrect It have two eyes.
✓ Correct It has two eyes.
✕ Incorrect It can to walk.
✓ Correct It can walk.
