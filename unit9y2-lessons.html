WELCOME TO UNIT 9

🏖️ Fun at the Beach!

Welcome to At the Beach! 🏖️ In this unit, we learn words and expressions about things we can do at the beach.

We will talk about beach activities, make suggestions and respond to our friends using simple English.

🎯 Learning Outcome 1: Identify and name common things and activities at the beach.
🎯 Learning Outcome 2: Talk about simple activities we can do at the beach.
🎯 Learning Outcome 3: Use Let's to make simple suggestions.
🎯 Learning Outcome 4: Respond to suggestions using Good idea!, I'm not sure. and Sorry, I don't want to.

03 Grammar

🗣️

Let's play

Use Let's to make a suggestion to do something together.

👍

Good idea!

Use this expression when you like and agree with a suggestion.

🤔

I'm not sure

Use this expression when you are not certain about a suggestion.

04 Let's

🗣️
Let's play

Use Let's to make a suggestion to do something together.

Let's + verb

We use Let's when we want to suggest an activity.
🏖️ Let's play!
🏰 Let's make a castle!
🐚 Let's look for shells!
📸 Let's take photos!
🍦 Let's eat ice cream!
👍
Good idea!

Use Good idea! when you agree with a suggestion and think it is a good thing to do.

Let's + activity
Good idea!
👧 Let's make a castle!
👦 Good idea!

👧 Let's take photos!
👦 Good idea!

👧 Let's eat ice cream!
👦 Good idea!
🤔
I'm not sure

Use I'm not sure when you are not certain or need some time to decide.

Let's + activity
I'm not sure.
👧 Let's catch fish!
👦 I'm not sure.

👧 Let's look for shells!
👦 I'm not sure.
🙅
Sorry, I don't want to.

Use Sorry, I don't want to. when you do not want to do the suggested activity.

Let's + activity
Sorry, I don't want to.
👧 Let's play in the sun!
👦 Sorry, I don't want to.

👧 Let's catch fish!
👦 Sorry, I don't want to.

10 Reading

🏖️ A Fun Day at the Beach
It is a sunny day.

Sam and Lily are at the beach. They can see the sea and the sun.

Lily says, “Let's make a castle!”
Sam says, “Good idea!”

They dig in the sand and make a big castle.

Lily says, “Let's look for shells!”
Sam says, “Good idea!”

They find some beautiful shells. Then they take photos.

They eat ice cream and play in the sun.

It is lots of fun!
