04 Can & Can't

🙋
I can / I can't

Use I can to say what you are able to do. Use I can't to say what you are not able to do.

Positive:
I can + verb.

Negative:
I can't + verb.
👍 I can walk.
👍 I can jump.
👍 I can run.

👎 I can't fly.
👎 I can't swim.
👦
He can / He can't

Use He can and He can't to talk about what a boy or man can do.

Positive:
He can + verb.

Negative:
He can't + verb.
👍 He can run.
👍 He can jump.
👍 He can play football.

👎 He can't fly.
👎 He can't swim.
👧
She can / She can't

Use She can and She can't to talk about what a girl or woman can do.

Positive:
She can + verb.

Negative:
She can't + verb.
👍 She can run.
👍 She can dance.
👍 She can jump.

👎 She can't fly.
👎 She can't swim.

05 Here • Here are • Here's

👉
Here

We use Here to show or point to something.

Here + is / are
👉 Here is the robot.
👉 Here is the head.
🤖
Here's an arm

Here's is short for Here is. We can use it when showing one thing.

Here's + a/an + singular noun
🤖 Here's an arm.
👂 Here's an ear.
👁️ Here's an eye.
👃 Here's a nose.
👀
Here are

Use Here are when showing two or more things.

Here are + plural noun
👀 Here are two eyes.
👂👂 Here are two ears.
🦾🦾 Here are two arms.
🦵🦵 Here are two legs.

06 Let's Make a Robot!

🤖
Let's make a robot

Use Let's when suggesting that we do something together.

Let's + verb
🤖 Let's make a robot!
✏️ Let's draw a robot!
🎨 Let's colour the robot!
👀 Let's add two eyes!
🦾 Let's add two arms!
