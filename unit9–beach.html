Put the words into a sentence.

Use beach vocabulary to describe what you can see around you.

I can see the sea and the waves.
02 · Activities

What can you do?

Talk about activities that people can do when they are at the beach.

Things you can do

Use can + base verb to talk about activities and abilities.

I can swim. 🏊

Things you can't do

Use can't + base verb when something is not possible.

I can't swim. 🚫
03 · Grammar

Look & Describe

Learn how to describe what someone is doing right now.

Present Continuous

What are they doing?

We use the present continuous to talk about something happening now.

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing
She is swimming. Dia sedang berenang.
He is playing with a ball. Dia sedang bermain dengan bola.
They are walking on the beach. Mereka sedang berjalan di pantai.
We are sitting on the sand. Kami sedang duduk di atas pasir.
04 · Sentence Builder

Build a Sentence

Combine the person, action and place to create a complete sentence.

Simple pattern

Start with who is doing the action, then add the action and place.

She is swimming in the sea.

06 · Accuracy

Common Mistakes

Small grammar mistakes can change the meaning of a sentence.

❌ She are swimming.
✓ She is swimming.
❌ They is playing.
✓ They are playing.
❌ I can swimming.
✓ I can swim.
❌ He can to swim.
✓ He can swim.
