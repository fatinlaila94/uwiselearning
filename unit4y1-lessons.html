04 Grammar Details

📦
The (lizard) is in/on/under the (bag).

Describe an animal’s position.

The + animal + is + in/on/under + the + object.
💬 The lizard is in the bag.
❤️
I like (dogs).

Say that you like an animal.

I like + plural animal.
💬 I like dogs.
🙅
I don’t like (dogs).

Say that you do not like an animal.

I don’t like + plural animal.
💬 I don’t like dogs.
